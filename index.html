The whole journey

How ClearPath takes you from application to certificate

01

Apply online

Submit your clearance request in one form — no printed forms, no queueing at the registrar's office.

02

Departments review

Finance, Library, Academics, Hostel, Games and the Registrar each approve or flag your request electronically.

03

Track & get notified

Real‑time alerts tell you the moment a department clears you — and automated reminders nudge any department that's slow.

04

Download your certificate

Once every department signs off, a QR‑verified clearance certificate is generated instantly for graduation.

Built for the whole campus

Everything the manual process couldn't do

📡

Real‑time notifications

Get pinged the moment a department approves, rejects, or needs more information from you.

⏰

Automated reminders

Departments sitting on a pending request get an automatic nudge so your file never goes cold.

🔳

QR‑verified certificates

Every finished clearance gets a unique QR code, so employers and the registrar can confirm it's genuine.

📊

Live progress tracker

See exactly which departments have cleared you and which are still pending — no more guessing.

🗂️

Secure digital records

Clearance history lives in one database instead of a paper file that can be lost or damaged.

📱

Works on any device

Apply, track and download your certificate from a phone, tablet or campus computer lab.
